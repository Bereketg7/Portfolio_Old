01 / About

Briefing

I'm an aircraft simulator engineer at CAE in Dallas. I troubleshoot, maintain and validate the full-flight simulators airline pilots train on.

My days are spent tracing faults across motion, visual, avionics and host systems, then writing them up so the next shift can move fast. Outside the sim bay I build full-stack web tools, and I'm studying for the FCC General Radiotelephone Operator License.
